Bill payments

Pay utility and service bills from your merchant account balance. Bill types vary by service provider.

Pay bills from your merchant balance

Bill payments is an add-on for electricity, water, TV, and similar bills. It generally does not incur transaction fees. Completed payments appear under Disbursements → Single Transactions.

What you can pay

Electricity, water, TV, and more. Offerings vary by service provider. Example: ESCOM Prepaid uses service code 101.

How it works

  1. Choose the service code
  2. Enter the service account number
  3. Provide transaction details
  4. Review the provider response

After you pay

A disbursement row appears under Disbursements → Single Transactions, labelled as a bill payment (for example ESCOM bill payment).

What to prepare

Gather these before you pay a bill from your merchant account balance. Bill payments generally do not incur transaction fees.

  • Sufficient balance in the merchant account
  • Service code for the provider (example: 101 for ESCOM Prepaid)
  • Service account number (example: meter number for ESCOM Prepaid)
  • Transaction details needed to complete the payment
  • After success, find the row under Disbursements → Single Transactions (labelled as a bill payment, for example ESCOM bill payment)
